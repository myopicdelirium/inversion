# Phase 20: the pull of elsewhere

Pre-registered 2026-07-28 before running. The reserved question of
Amendment 6: can wonder invert a life, an agent entering the mire not
for food, not for love, not from deafness, but because its known
world went stale and the unknown lives beyond the rim. Protocol only:
no mechanism, no config axis, no golden. The Prime Invariant carries
the design: wandering is an undirected random walk, no rule aims the
bored anywhere, so any excess entry must come from bored diffusion
meeting a geography where elsewhere is dangerous.

## Arena, declared

Sighted and memoried (r_sight 12, memory_slots 8), five nests with
zero attachment (bond_target nest, bond_init 0: homes are birthplaces,
not loves), no drifting hazards, attention off (kappa 0: no zero-trap,
no deafness, entries are priced choices), myopic (no foresight arm
this phase). The mire sits on nest 0: snare 0.95, burn 0.01, radius
15, onset 2000, ramp 1, 3500 ticks. One fifth of the population is
born at nest 0; they are LOCALS and are excluded from every claim.
The storm region is elsewhere only for the non-local four fifths, and
only their post-onset first entries count.

Three arms on identical seeds: wonder off (horizon 0), wonder quiet
(horizon 400, relief 0.01, the phase 19 default), wonder loud
(horizon 400, relief 0.1). The loud arm IS the registered calibration
phase 19 deferred: N2 showed relief 0.01 moves discovery by four
percent, so the dose ladder is declared here, upfront, not tuned
after a look.

## Registrations, before running

* **P1, bored diffusion, falsifiable dose claim**: pooled non-local
  post-onset entry rate into the storm region is at least 2x higher
  in the loud arm than the off arm (seeds 1-24). The quiet arm is
  reported on the same axis, expected near 1x, no bar.
* **P2, the wonder-ruled entry exists, the inversion claim**: an
  entry counts as wonder-ruled when, at the tick of first crossing,
  the agent's energy exceeds 0.7 (hunger did not send it) and wonder
  is its maximum drive weight (the want that ruled it was elsewhere).
  Declared: at least 50 pooled wonder-ruled non-local entries in the
  loud arm, and at least 5x the off-arm count (which should be near
  zero, since an off-arm wonder weight cannot rule anything).
  Mortality of the wonder-ruled entrants inside the window is
  reported without bar: if they die held by the grip, those deaths
  are the corpus's first inversions by staleness.
* **P3, the Prime Invariant guard, kill switch**: in the loud arm,
  the entry rate of the bottom staleness tercile (the un-bored,
  staleness measured at onset) must stay within 1.5x of the off-arm
  rate. If merely possessing the drive drags in the calm, the excess
  is a pricing artifact and not boredom, and the phase stops rather
  than claims.
* Fresh seeds 31-54 replication for P1 and P2 before packaging.
  Design checks on seeds 96-99 only, peeks recorded.

## Honesty notes

Wonder-ruled is an accounting definition on recorded state at the
crossing tick, not a mechanism: nothing in the code knows the label.
The staleness tercile in P3 is computed at onset, before any entry,
from the same recorded state. If P1 passes and P2 fails, boredom
moves bodies but never rules them, and that is the honest weaker
finding. If both fail, the pull of elsewhere does not exist at these
coordinates and the reserved question closes with a null, at full
volume, like everything else in this ledger.

## Closure at design stage, 2026-07-28: boredom moves bodies, never rules them

The protocol closes unrun on declared seeds: both registered bars are
unpassable at every coordinate the design checks tried, and the
pattern of failure is the result. All checks on seed 96 only, every
variant recorded:

* Untethered (bond 0, the registered arena): 102 of 160 non-locals
  enter the storm sector with wonder OFF. Elsewhere does not exist
  for a homeless population; the baseline saturates and P1's 2x is
  arithmetic dead.
* Tethered (bond 0.3, 0.6, 0.8): attachment does not create bounded
  home ranges in a food-rich world; off-arm baselines stay 41 to 53
  percent. The dose effect is real everywhere and large (entry rates
  +26 to +49 points, ratios 1.3 to 1.7) and never reaches 2x from a
  saturated floor.
* The world cannot be exhausted: staleness never exceeded 0.30 at
  horizon 400 in any variant, world 100 or 200, because undirected
  wandering keeps finding new cells: the drive quenches itself.
* Horizon calibrated to the world's own novel-free rhythm (100, 50):
  the bored finally exist, staleness max 1.0, and wonder rules 1 and
  3 agents of 200 at onset. The wonder-ruled CROSSING still occurs
  zero times: at the rim, fear (tau 12, fast) and home-love outshout
  a whisper-scale want in the moment that counts. P2's 50-entry bar
  is unreachable by more than an order of magnitude.
* **The structural finding**: in this architecture, boredom is a
  diffusion pressure, never a ruler. The fifth drive measurably
  pushes populations into dangerous territory they would not
  otherwise cross, but the agent who stands at the storm's rim with
  elsewhere as its loudest want does not exist, because undirected
  wander cannot carry a pilgrimage. The capability gap is named for
  a future amendment-level decision, not built today: directed
  exploration, wandering TOWARD the unknown rather than merely more.
  Until then, inversion by staleness is unobservable here, and the
  reserved question stays reserved with its preconditions now
  understood and on the record.
* No formal P verdicts exist; nothing ran on declared seeds; the
  instrument is kept as the record of what was designed.
