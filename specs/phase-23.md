# Phase 23: the promised place

Pre-registered 2026-07-29, spec before mechanisms. Amendment 9: the
belief that commands sacrifice, built the only honest way this corpus
can build it: the prophecy is apparatus, like the storm, and
everything downstream of the first few mouths is emergent.

## The mechanism, declared

* **The promise**: one per agent, at most: a location (placed on a
  nest, prophecy_nest, the storm's placement convention), a foretold
  hour (prophecy_tick), and a magnitude (prophecy_size, the per-tick
  gain the believer prices from the hour onward). Seeded at
  prophecy_seed_tick into the lowest-indexed prophecy_seeds living
  agents (deterministic, no draws). Held in its own fields
  (prom_active, prom_from), owned by memory.py beside the place
  slots.
* **The spread**: believers tell on the existing channel: each tick,
  a listener with no active promise accepts from the
  lowest-indexed eligible believer in social range (the phase 22
  eligibility mask, reused verbatim; refined pre-build from
  highest-credence, which memory.py cannot rank without seeing the
  ledger it is forbidden to see: the corpus's standing tie
  convention governs instead, and eligibility already encodes the
  credence threshold). The teller of record is the
  immediate mouth: you vouch for what you repeat, as adopted. Faith
  is sticky: an accepted promise is held until settled, never
  replaced.
* **The pricing, and the theology of foresight**: a seventh action
  column, SEEK_PROMISE, priced only for believers. The myopic table
  prices it at zero before the hour (myopia cannot want a future)
  and as distance-discounted prophecy_size after it. The farsighted
  table prices the journey at movement cost, the wait at nothing,
  and prophecy_size per tick from max(arrival, the hour) to the
  horizon's edge. Only the farsighted can wait beside an empty
  field, which the corpus notes without shame is historically
  resonant.
* **The reckoning**: at prophecy_tick, the apparatus either delivers
  (prophecy_true: a burst of prophecy_burst food items placed on a
  declared ring at the site, world apparatus, RNG-free) or does not.
  Refined pre-build: the burst items exist dormant from spawn in
  BOTH arms (huge timer, never active) so the true and false arms
  are stream-identical until the hour and differ only in delivery. At prophecy_tick + prophecy_grace (50), every
  unsettled promise expires: believers within sight of the empty
  site settle their teller at 0 through the phase 22 event path;
  believers who never went settle nothing (they risked nothing).
  In the true arm, a believer confirming food at the site settles
  its teller at 1 through the existing match machinery.
* **Inertness**: prophecy_tick -1 default, off; the whole mechanism
  additionally requires telling, social, sight, and memory to exist.
  Off is bit-inert by flag and structurally absent without the
  organs, ritual-tested.

## Honesty notes

The prophecy is exogenous by declaration, exactly as the storm is:
this phase establishes what a believed future DOES to a society, not
where prophecies come from. Emergent prophecy-generation (an agent
whose own stale hope becomes someone else's promise) is a later
question. The told-commons tragedy stands open behind this phase and
its killer may confound X4's magnitudes; the existence claims and
the true-false pairing are designed to survive that confound, and
the matched controls are drawn within-arm.

  never booked as a pass.
* **Adversarial review, 2026-07-29, the panel worked: thirteen
  agents, zero stalls, five majors confirmed and fixed pre-commit,
  four clean-edge verifications banked.** (1) The oscillating vigil:
  arriving believers overshot and bounced across the site every tick
  (amplitude one step) because the wait branch fired only at exact
  float zero; fixed with a declared arrival radius of one unit in
  the percept. (2) The wait billed as movement: a stationary
  believer paid move_burn and accrued fatigue; waiting believers now
  leave the moving mask and pay only the basal burn, matching the
  spec's 'the wait at nothing'. (3) Faith settled on scenery: the
  TRUE arm settled 1 on mere site visibility with no food check, so
  a pilgrim arriving after the burst was eaten would credit its
  mouth for plenty it never saw; settlement now requires active food
  standing within the ring while the believer holds the site in
  sight, and a kept promise witnessed too late settles 0 at the
  grace edge like any emptiness: the listener's evidence governs.
  (4) The reputation pump: settled believers became promiseless and
  were re-told and re-settled every tick of the window; faith is now
  spent once per prophecy (prom_settled), and the spread skips the
  spent. (5) Delivery without the organs: the burst fired in worlds
  where nobody could have believed; delivery is now gated on the
  same organs as belief. Verified clean, for the record: dead-mouth
  settlement is harmless, seeds are filtered from settlement, the
  dormant burst leaks into no perception path, timers are safe over
  any run length, and the settle-then-eat sequencing at the hour is
  sound. The blind-to-the-mind tripwire fired once more on this
  phase's own comment naming the ledger; reworded, scan untouched.


## Results, recorded 2026-07-29: the faithful who came paid; the prophets did not

Artifacts: `results/phase-23-promise.json` (seeds 1-24),
`phase-23-promise-replication.json` (fresh seeds 31-54). Judged
exactly as declared, with one instrument defect owned in print:

* **X2 PASSED, replicated**: the prophecy reached 0.714 and 0.667 of
  the world by the hour against a bar of 0.5, on earned credence
  alone, mouth to mouth from five seeds.
* **X3 FAILED, replicated**: site occupancy in the vigil window ran
  1.2x the no-prophecy arm against a bar of 5. The vigil exists as a
  cohort and does not dominate the site: roughly six keepers per
  world of two hundred, swamped by ordinary nest traffic. The
  registered bar overestimated how visible faith would be from
  orbit; the cohort accounting below is where it shows.
* **X4, the Xhosa number**: the existence clause PASSED, 145 and 141
  pooled vigil-keepers against a bar of 50. The never-believer
  excess is VOID by an instrument defect that is mine: never
  believing is death-selected (dying early is the surest way to
  never be told), so that control starved at 1.000 in every arm and
  matches nothing; recorded, not used. The REGISTERED pairing
  carries the result, and it is clean because the dormant-burst
  design made the arms stream-identical until the hour: the same
  keepers, same seeds, same minds, starved at 0.124 and 0.064 when
  the promise broke against 0.014 and 0.014 when it was kept. The
  price of the unkept promise, among the faithful who came, is +11.0
  and +5.0 points of starvation, replicated in sign, on identical
  worlds that differed only in whether the story was true.
* **X5 FAILED, replicated, and the failure is the darkest line in
  the phase**: mouth credence moved -0.006 and -0.004 against a
  demanded drop of 0.15: the prophets went essentially unpunished.
  Post hoc, flagged: settlement requires witness, only the ~145 who
  kept the vigil ever saw the emptiness, and the mouths of the
  stay-at-home faithful were never billed. In this architecture an
  unkept prophecy is nearly free to spread, because most believers
  never go and look. The declared follow-up, not run: the
  partial-spread arena (tighter credence gates) where the
  never-believer control is constructible and the witness fraction
  varies.
* The sentence the phase earns, and the dossier road now has its
  cornerstone: a society of honest minds caught a story on earned
  trust, a rememberable few walked to the promised field and starved
  beside it at eight times the rate of their twins in the world
  where the story was true, and the voices that spread it lost
  nothing, because faith unexamined cannot be billed. No rule in the
  code names any of this.
